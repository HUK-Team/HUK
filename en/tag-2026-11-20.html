Question 20: What does the Holy scripture say about Jesus Christ as the Lord. Jesus
Christ is the Lord, He is the salvation, He is the authority of God. This almighty power of God will bless us unto eternal power. Jesus Christ must be revealed through our lifestyle today and display the Laws of the love of God. We need to sacrifice to give ourselves for salvation of others.
